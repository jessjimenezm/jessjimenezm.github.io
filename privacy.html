Privacy policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

The short version

Jess Between Moms is a small app made by one mom. It collects as little as it can, never sells your data, and has no ads.

Most of what you enter stays on your phone. If you sign in, it’s also backed up to your account so you can use it on another phone. You can delete your account and everything in it from Profile at any time.

What stays on your phone

Your pump log, baby’s feeds, diapers and sleep, milk plan, freezer stash, baby’s birthday or due date, saved picks, reminders and settings are saved on your phone.

If you connect Apple Health, the app reads your sleep and water on your phone to show what might be helping your supply. Apple Health data is never sent to us, never backed up, and never used for anything else.

What we keep if you sign in

Your email address (used only to sign you in), the name you choose to show, and a backup of the on-phone data listed above (except Apple Health data).

Anything you post in Community: posts, replies, photos, likes, and any reports or blocks you make. Posts, replies and photos can be seen by anyone using the app, with the name you chose.

If you turn on notifications (replies or new posts from Jess), a notification token for your phone and your language.

Other things we collect

Newsletter: if you sign up, your email address and language, used only to send the newsletter.

Usage counts: which screens get opened, links tapped (picks, blog posts, guides) and invites sent, with the time. They aren’t linked to you or your phone.

Crash reports: if the app crashes, a report with technical details (phone model, app version, what went wrong) is sent to our crash reporting service, Sentry. It doesn’t include your pump log or posts.

Services we use

Supabase stores accounts, Community and backups. Expo delivers app updates and notifications. Sentry receives crash reports. Apple handles Between Moms+ payments, and RevenueCat keeps track of whether you have Between Moms+ (it gets an anonymous ID and your purchase, not your name or pump log).

Some picks are affiliate links (for example Amazon). When you open one, you leave the app and that store’s own privacy policy applies. As an Amazon Associate, Jess earns from qualifying purchases.

Deleting your data

Profile → Delete account removes your account, your posts, replies, photos, likes, backup and notification tokens right away.

Deleting the app removes what’s saved on your phone. To leave the newsletter, write to us at jessbetweenmoms@gmail.com.

Children

The app is for parents and caregivers, not for children. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app with a new date.

Questions or requests: jessbetweenmoms@gmail.com.
